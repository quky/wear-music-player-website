Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

Wear Music Player is an Android and Wear OS application that lets users manage music on their phone and transfer music to a compatible smartwatch for playback. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when using the application.

1. Music Files and Local Storage

Wear Music Player accesses audio files and music folders selected or authorized by the user. Music files are used for browsing, playlist management, and transfer to the watch. Transfers take place directly between the phone and watch using the Wear OS connection or local Wi-Fi.

Music files are not uploaded to our cloud servers. Transferred music is stored locally on the watch.

2. Information Collected

Depending on the features used, the application and integrated services may process:

  • Anonymous authentication identifiers used for support services.
  • Optional support display names and messages.
  • Optional screenshots or images attached to support messages.
  • Device information, app usage information, and analytics events.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging registration tokens used for support notifications.
  • Crash diagnostics, including error details, device model, Android version, and technical logs, when submitted by the user.

3. Support Chat and Attachments

When you use the in-app support feature, messages and information you provide are transmitted to Firebase services operated by Google. Optional image attachments may be stored in Firebase Storage. This information is used to respond to support requests and investigate reported problems.

4. Crash Diagnostics

The application may save crash information locally to help diagnose unexpected failures. Users can review available crash reports and choose whether to submit them to the developer through Firebase services. Crash reports may include technical information about the device and application.

5. Analytics and Notifications

Wear Music Player includes Firebase Analytics, which may collect app interaction and device information to help understand application usage and improve reliability. Firebase Cloud Messaging is used to deliver support-related notifications where enabled.

6. Permissions

The application may request access to audio files, Bluetooth or nearby devices, network connectivity, notifications, and other system capabilities needed for music transfer, playback, and support features. Permissions depend on the device and Android version.

7. Third-Party Services

The application uses Google services, including Firebase Authentication, Firestore, Cloud Storage, Analytics, Cloud Messaging, App Check, and Google Play services. These services may process information according to their own privacy policies.

See the Google Privacy Policy.

8. Data Security, Retention, and Deletion

We use platform security features and Firebase services to help protect information. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

Support messages, attachments, and submitted diagnostics may be retained as needed to provide support, investigate technical problems, and maintain the service.

How to request data deletion: You may request deletion of your support-related information at any time by emailing hidroela@gmail.com. Use the subject "Wear Music Player - Data Deletion Request" and include enough information to help us identify your support conversation. Do not include passwords or other sensitive information.

Data that will be deleted: After verifying your request, we will delete the associated support messages, optional support display name, uploaded screenshots and attachments, and submitted crash reports from our Firebase services, where applicable.

Processing time: Verified deletion requests will be processed within 30 days.

Data that may be retained: Certain information may be retained for longer only where necessary to comply with legal obligations or legitimate security requirements. Any such retention will be limited to the information and period necessary for that purpose.

Music files and playlists: Your music files and playlists remain on your phone or watch and are not stored on our cloud servers. You can remove them directly from your devices.

9. Children's Privacy

Wear Music Player is not specifically directed toward children. If you believe a child has submitted personal information through the support feature, please contact us so the request can be reviewed.

10. Your Choices

You can choose which music folders and files to access, whether to send support messages or attachments, and whether to submit available crash reports. You can manage applicable app permissions and notifications through Android settings.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when application functionality or data practices change. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised date.

12. Contact

For privacy questions, support inquiries, or requests concerning your information, contact: hidroela@gmail.com.
